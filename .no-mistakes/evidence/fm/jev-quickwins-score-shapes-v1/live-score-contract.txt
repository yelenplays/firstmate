--- B. The documented shape this change now sends (ordered criteria, 5 levels) ---
== criteria ==
exit=0 http=200 model=jev-latest
stderr: 
{"model":"jev-1.13.0","answers":{"strength":{"type":"score","score":3.42,"confidence":0.52,"legend":{"0":"Guess: the evidence barely bears on the verdict.","1":"Weak: the evidence leans toward the verdict but leaves it open.","2":"Moderate: the evidence supports the verdict with notable gaps.","3":"Strong: the evidence supports the verdict with only minor gaps.","4":"Clear: the evidence plainly matches or plainly contradicts the claim."},"probabilities":{"0":0.0,"1":0.02,"2":0.09,"3":0.35,"4":0.54}}}}

The criteria run weakest -> strongest ("Guess" .. "Clear") and the API returns
score 3.42, a probability-weighted LEVEL INDEX on the 0..4 scale, not a 0..1
value. That is why both builders now divide the index by the top level
(length-1).
